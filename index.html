Privacy Policy – Find It!

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Find It! is a mobile puzzle game developed by Beyzoli Games.

Information We Collect

Find It! does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Internet Connection

Find It! does not require an internet connection to play. The game does not send information to external servers.

Advertising

Find It! does not currently contain advertisements.

Analytics and Tracking

Find It! does not use analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party services to track users.

Local Game Data

The game may store game-related information, such as the player's high score, locally on the device. This information is not transmitted to Beyzoli Games or any third party.

Children's Privacy

Because Find It! does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the features or data practices of Find It! change. Any updates will be reflected on this page.
